# SOC Phishing Simulation

## Overview

This room provided hands-on experience with phishing investigation workflows commonly performed by Security Operations Center (SOC) analysts. The exercise involved analyzing suspicious emails, identifying potential phishing indicators, and investigating artifacts associated with phishing campaigns.

## Objectives

* Understand the phishing investigation process
* Identify suspicious characteristics within emails
* Analyze indicators associated with phishing attacks
* Develop a structured approach to alert triage and investigation

## Skills Developed

* Phishing detection
* Email analysis
* IOC identification
* Threat investigation
* SOC analyst workflows

## Key Takeaways

This room helped reinforce the importance of recognizing phishing techniques, validating indicators, and following a systematic investigation process to determine whether an email represents a legitimate threat.
