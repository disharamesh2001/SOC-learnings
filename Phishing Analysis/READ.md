# Phishing Analysis

## Overview

This learning path focused on analyzing, investigating, and defending against phishing attacks through hands-on exercises and real-world scenarios. The module covered email fundamentals, phishing indicators, investigation techniques, phishing analysis tools, prevention strategies, and practical case studies involving malicious emails and URLs.

Through multiple investigations, I developed the ability to identify phishing attempts, analyze suspicious email artifacts, and apply structured investigation methodologies commonly used by Security Operations Center (SOC) analysts.

## Objectives

## Challenges Faced

### Identifying Sophisticated Phishing Attempts

Some phishing emails closely resembled legitimate communications, making it difficult to distinguish malicious content from genuine messages.

### Investigating Multiple Indicators

Each investigation involved numerous artifacts such as sender addresses, domains, URLs, attachments, and email headers.

### Correlating Evidence

Determining whether multiple indicators were related required careful analysis and validation.

### Understanding Email Infrastructure

Analyzing email headers and message routing required familiarity with email protocols and authentication mechanisms.

## How I Overcame Them

### Following a Structured Investigation Process

Approached each investigation systematically by examining sender information, email content, links, attachments, and technical indicators.

### Validating Indicators Individually

Analyzed each IOC independently before correlating findings to establish the overall threat context.

### Leveraging Threat Intelligence

Used reputation and intelligence sources to validate suspicious domains, URLs, and email addresses.

### Applying Defensive Analysis Techniques

Focused on identifying behavioral indicators and attack patterns commonly associated with phishing campaigns.

## Key Takeaways

* Phishing attacks remain one of the most common initial access techniques used by threat actors.
* Email headers provide valuable information for validating message authenticity.
* Indicators such as suspicious URLs, spoofed domains, and unusual sender behavior can reveal phishing attempts.
* Structured investigation workflows improve accuracy and reduce false positives.
* Effective phishing prevention requires a combination of user awareness, technical controls, and analyst investigations.
* Threat intelligence significantly enhances phishing analysis and incident response efforts.
